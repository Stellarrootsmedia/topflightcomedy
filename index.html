About the founder

Meet Evan Lopez

Evan Lopez is a seasoned Austin comedian and the founder of Top Flight Comedy. Born in a small desert town and raised in a diverse household, his blend of cultures fuels a sharp, authentic stand-up style — and he's been featured on OFTV's hit “LMAOF” series.

He founded Top Flight Comedy with a clear mission: to craft high-quality, efficiently produced comedy shows for audiences and businesses alike. Drawing on years of hosting, booking, performing, and producing, Evan handles every detail so clients can simply relax and enjoy the entertainment.

“Inspired by the freedom of flight, we bring the best comedians together to keep crowds soaring with laughter.”
