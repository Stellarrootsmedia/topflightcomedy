Why the early show

The Early Show is built for people who would rather laugh at 6 than wait for a 10PM start. Catch the show, then head to dinner on East 6th or out for the rest of your night.

The lineup is stacked with comics who play the Comedy Mothership, Moontower, Cap City Comedy Club, The Creek & The Cave and Kill Tony, plus nationally touring headliners, comics featured on OFTV and Skankfest regulars.

Downtown clubs usually mean a higher ticket price, paid parking and a two-drink minimum. This show is $15 with no drink minimum, and there's convenient street parking around the club.

Who runs it

The Early Show is produced by Top Flight Comedy, founded by Austin comedian Evan Lopez. Want Friday instead? Daywalkers runs at 6PM every Friday. For a midweek show, try Hump Day Haha's on Wednesdays at 8PM.
