What to expect

Hump Day Haha's is Top Flight Comedy's Wednesday night stand-up show. Every lineup mixes Austin's sharpest local comics with national touring acts passing through town, so no two shows are the same.

The room is small and intimate. Seats are limited, so grab tickets ahead of time. Doors open 30 minutes before showtime.

Make a night of it on East 6th Street. There are plenty of places to grab food before or after the show, and parking is free.

Who runs it

The show is produced by Top Flight Comedy, the Austin comedy production company founded by comedian Evan Lopez. Looking for an earlier night? Try Daywalkers on Fridays or the Saturday Early Show, both at 6PM.
