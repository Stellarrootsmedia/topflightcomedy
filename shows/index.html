Comedy in Austin, produced by Top Flight

Every show on this page is produced by Top Flight Comedy and hosted at East Austin Comedy Club, 2505 East 6th Street, Suite D. The room is small, so you're close enough to see every reaction, and lineups change every show.

Top Flight was founded by Austin stand-up comedian Evan Lopez, who books, hosts and produces the shows.

Top Flight also produces corporate comedy, private speakeasy nights and recurring shows for venues.
